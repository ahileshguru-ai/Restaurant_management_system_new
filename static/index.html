🔄 Single-Seat Handover Protocol

Jim Gray & David Ferraiolo Handover Specification:
1. Hard DELETE outgoing credentials from Users table.
2. Soft-retire outgoing manager in Staff (IsActive = 0) to preserve historical audits.
3. INSERT incoming employee into Staff.
4. INSERT new credentials into Users pointing to new EmployeeID.
